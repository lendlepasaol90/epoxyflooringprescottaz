Request a free estimate or ask a question about your floor. We will be in touch to discuss it.

Helpful details to include

  • The space you would like coated and its approximate size
  • The age of the home or building, and any previous coatings or paint
  • Visible cracks, flaking or stains
  • Your preferred timing, and any historic district or HOA considerations
