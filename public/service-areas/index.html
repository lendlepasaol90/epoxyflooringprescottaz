Where we work

Prescott and the communities of central Yavapai County, with commercial and hangar projects further afield by arrangement.

Around the Granite Dells

Coating work depends on timing. Temperatures, monsoon storms and the occasional winter snow all affect when a floor can be prepared and coated. Keeping our work concentrated around Prescott lets us respond to the weather and keep projects on schedule.

These are the communities we serve most often. If yours is not listed, please telephone; we will tell you honestly whether we can help.

Prescott

Every part of the city, from homes near Courthouse Square and Mt. Vernon Street to Prescott Lakes, Hassayampa Village, Yavapai Hills, the Ranch and the neighborhoods out toward Willow and Watson Lakes. Commercial and hangar work throughout.

Prescott Valley

Garages, interiors and businesses just east of Prescott, including newer homes where builder sealers must be removed before coating.

Chino Valley

Homes, shops and new construction to the north, often with detached garages and workshops.

Williamson Valley

Ranch properties, barns, equipment buildings and detached garages along Williamson Valley Road.

Groom Creek

Cabins and homes in the pines south of town, where cold, shaded slabs and seasonal moisture require careful planning.

Skull Valley

Rural homes and outbuildings to the west, by arrangement.

Dewey-Humboldt

Larger lots with shops and garages along Highway 69.

Kirkland

Ranch and rural properties further southwest, scheduled by arrangement.

Services offered throughout the area

  • Garage Floor Coatings: Attached garages, carriage houses and collector garages
  • Residential Epoxy Flooring: Basements, laundry rooms, studios and casitas
  • Decorative Flake Flooring: Granite-look chip floors in local palettes
  • Metallic Epoxy Flooring: Hand-worked floors for galleries, homes and showrooms
  • Polyaspartic Floor Coatings: Rapid-curing, light-stable topcoats
  • Polyurea Floor Coatings: Resilient base coats for barns, shops and older slabs
  • Patio & Concrete Coatings: Porches, front walks, steps and patios
  • Commercial Epoxy Flooring: Restaurants, galleries, shops and offices
  • Hangar & Warehouse Coatings: Aircraft hangars, maintenance shops and warehouses
